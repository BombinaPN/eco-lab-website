  <p class="lec-home-kicker">Theses · Internships · Field training</p>
  <h2>Opportunities for BSc, MSc Theses and Internships</h2>
  <p>The <strong>LEC - Lab for Ecology & Conservation</strong> regularly offers opportunities for BSc and MSc theses, as well as curricular internships, within its ongoing research and conservation projects.</p>
  <p>Students may be involved in fieldwork, ecological monitoring, data collection and analysis, wildlife surveys, statistical analyses, and the development of independent research projects. Activities are often carried out in collaboration with research institutions, protected areas, and national and international conservation partners.</p>
</div>

<div class="lec-opportunities-grid">
  <section class="lec-opportunity-card">
    <p class="lec-opportunity-label">Island conservation</p>
    <h2>Conservation of the Aeolian Wall Lizard</h2>
    <p>Within the framework of the European project <strong>LIFE EOLIZARD</strong>, dedicated to the conservation of the Aeolian wall lizard (<em>Podarcis raffonei</em>), thesis and internship opportunities are available on a variety of topics related to island ecology and conservation biology.</p>
    <h3>Potential topics</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>population monitoring and demographic analyses;</li>
      <li>spatial ecology and habitat use;</li>
      <li>impacts of invasive species on island biodiversity;</li>
      <li>evaluation of conservation actions and management strategies;</li>
      <li>ecological and demographic data analysis;</li>
      <li>science communication and conservation outreach.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Activities may include fieldwork in the Aeolian Islands and direct participation in conservation actions implemented within the LIFE project.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="lec-opportunity-card">
    <p class="lec-opportunity-label">Movement ecology</p>
    <h2>Biologging and Animal Tracking in Amphibians and Reptiles</h2>
    <p>The lab develops and applies innovative biologging and animal-tracking techniques to investigate the ecology and behaviour of amphibians and reptiles.</p>
    <h3>Potential activities</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>VHF radiotelemetry;</li>
      <li>use of accelerometers, magnetometers and GPS devices;</li>
      <li>movement ecology and space-use analyses;</li>
      <li>activity patterns and behavioural ecology;</li>
      <li>development and validation of tracking methodologies for small vertebrates;</li>
      <li>applications of biologging technologies to wildlife conservation.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Students will have the opportunity to acquire practical and analytical skills that are increasingly valuable in the fields of movement ecology, wildlife monitoring and conservation science.</p>
  </section>
</div>
