  <h2>From field research to conservation action</h2>
  <p>The <strong>LEC - Lab for Ecology & Conservation</strong> studies the ecological processes shaping the distribution, abundance and functioning of animal communities, with a special focus on amphibians and reptiles, threatened species and invasive species.</p>
  <p>We combine basic and applied research to turn field data, laboratory work and innovative monitoring technologies into evidence-based tools for biodiversity conservation.</p>

## What We Do

<div class="lec-home-card-grid">
  <div class="lec-home-card">
    <h3>Ecology and conservation</h3>
    <p>We study animal populations and communities to understand how they respond to environmental change, habitat fragmentation, invasive species and human-driven pressures.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="lec-home-card">
    <h3>Monitoring and biologging</h3>
    <p>We use VHF telemetry, GPS, accelerometers, magnetometers and molecular tools such as eDNA to collect high-resolution data on movements, activity and space use.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="lec-home-card">
    <h3>Threatened species</h3>
    <p>We work on amphibians and reptiles of conservation concern, contributing to monitoring protocols, active management and the assessment of conservation actions.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="lec-home-card">
    <h3>Collaborations</h3>
    <p>We collaborate with universities, research institutes, protected areas, local authorities and national and international partners in research and conservation projects.</p>
  </div>
</div>
